# What's new

Each version's list is what the app shows in "Update available" and on the
release page: short points, written for the people using the app.

## 0.7.1

- Fixed: on some Windows computers with Intel graphics the app did not open.
- The menus are now in the top bar of the window on Windows and Linux.
- An update can be installed now or later, when it suits you.
- A clear message when GitHub has had too many update checks from your network.

## 0.7.0

- Repair videos that will not play because the recording was cut off (battery, full card), with the help of a good video from the same camera.
- Open disks of virtual machines (VHD, VHDX, VMDK, VDI, QCOW2) and forensic images (E01) like any disk image.
- Recover from NAS drives (Synology, QNAP): their RAID and volumes are put back together, even with a drive missing.
- Combine several drives of a RAID into one with "Combine drives (RAID)…".
- Read Linux btrfs and XFS drives with their names and folders, deleted files included.
- A warning when a drive reports that it is failing, or when parts of it cannot be read.
